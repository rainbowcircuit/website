Rainbow Circuit ideates, codes and publishes instruments that propel creative technology, multiply musical colors, and realize new potential for form.

We're interested in the design choices that charge an instrument with potential: choices that suggest anew and elevate the experience of making music.

Emphasis is placed on both form and function — an instrument should sound fantastic, and its visual form also be designed to be understood.

Rainbow Circuit is Takuma Matsui, a designer and musician based in Providence, Rhode Island.
